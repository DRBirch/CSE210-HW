This program would be used for operating devices in your home from a device like a laptop or phone. To use it is fairly self explanatory but once you turn it on there is a menu that will offer the devices you have access to and the changes you wish to make regarding them.

1: A change in the lighting. You can change the brightness by putting in a number between 1 and 100 to choose the level you wish.
2: A change in the thermostat. Similar to the lighting you input a number which will control the thermostat changing the temperature to the desired input
3: Locking or unlocking the door. Once selected you will be prompted for input. To lock you must put "Lock" it is case sensitive, anything else will leave the door unlocked or unlock the door.
4: Roomba vaccuming or returning to the charging port. Once selected you will be prompted for input. To start cleaning you must put "Start cleaning" it is case sensitive, anything else will leave the roomba at the port or return it to the port. 
5: Security camera recording or stop recording. Once selected you will be prompted for input. To start recording you must put "Record" it is case sensitive, anything else will leave the camera off or turn it off.
6: Alarm turning on or off. Once selected you will be prompted for input. To turn the alarm on you must put "on" it is case sensitive, anything else will leave the alarm off or turn it off.
You may also use the seventh option to display the status of each device
